# Rock-sampling activities - v3 review

Prepared 2026-11-15 on `feature/science-suggestions` for local testing.

## What is delivered

Four project-proposed rock-chip collection activities near the existing Confidence
Hills, Mojave 2, Telegraph Peak and Buckskin study locations. They reuse the
reviewed historical rover-position proxies and mineral evidence. No new sampling
coordinates, rock availability, mineral measurements or NASA activity records are
generated.

These activities supplement the photography options. There are now 14 activities:
ten existing imaging follow-ups/candidates and four sampling proposals, under the
same three objectives. Confidence Hills sampling also supports the past-water
objective; all four sampling proposals support mineral diversity.

## Equipment and proposed collection plan

Each sampling activity requires:

- Field camera for context documentation.
- Rock sampling tools: geology hammer and rock-handling tools.
- Labelled sample containers for individual containment and identification.

Close-up imaging optics remain required for the four existing close-up photography
activities, but are not a prerequisite for this context-photography sampling plan.
All four selectable equipment capabilities are scenario assumptions. Tool mass,
container capacity and contamination-control performance are unspecified.

The procedure is a **project-authored, source-informed plan**:

1. Photograph and describe the selected rock in context.
2. If suitable material is confirmed on site, obtain a chip with sampling tools.
3. Individually contain and identify it, recording site and photo references.
4. Stow the documented sample for later laboratory analysis.

This is not an instruction to recover historical drill powder. A new chip need
not represent the material analyzed by Curiosity. Its mineral identity and
abundance remain undetermined until a suitable laboratory analysis is performed.
No laboratory instrument or automated mineral result is added to this release.

## Application behavior

Refresh localhost and open Science. Choose Investigate mineral diversity; each
historical sample location now has photography and collection options. A sampling
card is disabled until camera, tools and containers are selected. Its Why this
stop section contains the collection plan, planned output and primary sources.

Photography and sampling at the same grid location reuse one route stop. The
Science missions switch still controls the evidence overlays as one layer. A
location popup lists both experiments rather than stacking duplicate markers.
Removing one activity retains a shared stop; removing its last activity removes
the stop if it was generated by these activities.

The current time-per-stop setting applies once per physical stop. It is not a
validated sampling duration, and multiple activities at a stop do not add an
invented time increment. There is no new payload, oxygen or sample-capacity model.

Mission save/reopen and JSON exports retain the procedure, equipment, references
and original evidence. Printable reports show the collection plan and the later
analysis outcome. Earlier saved collections remain embedded in their missions.
